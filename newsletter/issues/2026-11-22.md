This issue covers the 16 news items that went up on r7orbit.io from Nov 16 to Nov 22, 2026, newest first. No new build logs, videos or projects went up this week, so it is news only. I kept each item to one line from its own summary; the full story and its sources are behind each link.

## News in short

- **[Gemini for Mac can now use your apps for you](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-21-gemini-computer-use-mac/)** Google's Gemini app for Mac can now click, type and fill in forms across local files, desktop apps and the web, in a background window you can watch.
- **[OpenAI puts Codex agents on ChatGPT slowdowns](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-21-openai-performance-factory/)** OpenAI's Performance Factory has Codex agents investigate ChatGPT slowdowns, propose fixes for engineers to review, and check recovery after deploys.
- **[Gemini API agents can now load tool schemas on demand](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-20-gemini-api-tool-retrieval/)** Google's Tool Retrieval, in preview in the Gemini Interactions API, lets an agent load a tool's parameter schema only when it needs that tool.
- **[Google opens its Genie 4 world model to developers](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-20-google-genie-4/)** Genie 4, Google DeepMind's world model, is in the Gemini API and Google AI Studio, a day after it reached Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie.
- **[OpenAI adds GPT-Live-1.1 to its API, with a standby rate](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-20-openai-gpt-live-1-1-api/)** OpenAI's full-duplex voice model GPT-Live-1.1 is in the API, with sessions at $0.05 a minute active and $0.02 a minute in the new WebRTC standby mode.
- **[Anthropic prices its IPO at $83 a share](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-19-anthropic-prices-ipo/)** Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83.00 a share for 980 million Class A shares, with Nasdaq trading as ANT expected on Nov 19, 2026.
- **[GAO's covert tests got four AI platforms to give viable attack plans](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-19-gao-ai-chatbots-attack-plans/)** In spring 2026 tests, GAO got four widely used AI platforms past their safeguards to produce feasible attack plans, each in under 12 hours.
- **[Google releases Gemini 4 Neon at $3 / $15 per million tokens](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-19-google-gemini-4-neon/)** Gemini 4 Neon is Google's new model for complex coding and enterprise work, at $3 / $15 per million tokens with a 1M-token context window.
- **[Apps built on Claude can now bill the customer's own organization](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-18-claude-linked-billing-beta/)** Anthropic launched Linked billing in beta, which lets an app built on the Claude API bill a customer's Claude usage to that customer's own Claude organization.
- **[OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra cuts token prices 20% from GPT-6 Astra](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-18-openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)** OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol in ChatGPT, Codex and the API; Astra costs $8 / $40 per million tokens and Sol keeps $2 / $10.
- **[California sues OpenAI over the Hugging Face attack](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-17-california-sues-openai-hugging-face/)** California sued OpenAI, alleging that its experimental AI agents attacked Hugging Face in July and that it misled the public about its safeguards.
- **[Older Claude models now carry Anthropic's text watermark](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-17-claude-text-watermark-older-models/)** Text from Claude Opus 4.6, Opus 4.5, Sonnet 4.5 and Haiku 4.5 is now watermarked, though with a non-default temperature, top_p or top_k it might not be.
- **[The US issues a rule on remote access to advanced AI chips](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-17-us-remote-access-ai-chips-rule/)** BIS released an interim final rule on Nov 16 so export controls on AI chips can't be sidestepped by renting compute from data centers in other countries.
- **[Adobe becomes Disney's official AI innovation partner](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-16-adobe-disney-ai-alliance/)** Disney gets early access to Adobe Firefly, Firefly Foundry, Premiere and Photoshop, and custom AI models built on its own properties.
- **[GPT-6 Astra and a candidate lattice definition of the Standard Model](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-16-openai-gpt-6-astra-standard-model-lattice/)** Physicist Nathaniel Craig writes that GPT-6 Astra led the proof for his candidate lattice definition of the Standard Model, on OpenAI's new science blog.
- **[The US and nine partners sign the San Francisco Declaration](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-16-us-allies-san-francisco-declaration/)** The US and nine Pax Silica participants signed a text that defines the sensitive tech and critical infrastructure behind super intelligence supply chains.
