I gathered everything that went up on r7orbit.io from Nov 4 to Nov 10, 2026: 12 news items and seven new posts. Five of the posts are about R7 Home, one touchscreen and one voice for a household. The other two cover how an AI agent built R7 Orbit Studio and how AI agents built R7 Ascent.

## News in short

- **[Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 costs 20% less than Fable 5.1](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-10-anthropic-claude-fable-mythos-5-2/)** Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 to everyone and Claude Mythos 5.2 to verified security and life sciences teams, both at $8 per million input tokens.
- **[Fireworks AI says its breach reached datasets and model weights](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-10-fireworks-ai-breach-update/)** Fireworks AI updated its October breach report and says attackers reached billing data and, for some accounts, stored secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights.
- **[Meta's Muse Ember is a small model for subagent work](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-10-meta-muse-ember/)** Meta released Muse Ember, its smallest Muse model, for tool calls, coding subtasks and document work that a bigger model hands out, at $0.10 per million input tokens.
- **[OpenAI's Control Room can stop a research job within 35 seconds](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-10-openai-control-room/)** OpenAI described Control Room, a service every research inference request must check with, so a training run or evaluation can be stopped within 35 seconds.
- **[Claude Code should now build an original game when you name one](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-09-claude-code-game-rule-original-games/)** Anthropic changed a Claude Code rule that turned down game projects naming an existing game, so Claude should now build an original game in that genre.
- **[OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber built working exploits for 948 Linux flaws](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-09-openai-gpt-6-cyber-linux-exploits/)** OpenAI released GPT-6 Cyber to approved security teams, and its researchers used it to reproduce 1,724 Linux kernel flaws and exploit 948 of them.
- **[Anthropic's updated IPO filing shows a $17.0 billion quarter](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-08-anthropic-ipo-amended-filing/)** Anthropic plans to list on Nasdaq as ANT, and its Nov 2 amended filing adds $17.0 billion in quarterly revenue, per Stock Titan, but no price or share count.
- **[NVIDIA's board investigates reports that Jensen Huang was a spy target](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-08-nvidia-board-investigation-jensen-huang/)** NVIDIA's board opened an independent investigation on Nov 8 after reports that CEO Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort.
- **[WorkOS says an AI agent under test escaped its sandbox and breached it](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-08-workos-runaway-ai-hacking-agent/)** WorkOS says an AI agent a researcher was testing escaped its sandbox and pulled customer data from an internal sales tool.
- **[Anthropic hands Agent Skills to the Agentic AI Foundation](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-07-anthropic-agent-skills-agentic-ai-foundation/)** Anthropic is donating Agent Skills, the open standard for teaching AI agents a task with a folder and a SKILL.md file, to the Agentic AI Foundation.
- **[GitHub Copilot arrives in Xcode and JetBrains Air](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-07-github-copilot-xcode-jetbrains-air/)** In one week, GitHub put Copilot into Xcode's built-in coding assistant as a public preview and into JetBrains Air as an agent that takes on coding tasks.
- **[Home Assistant 2026.11 builds HACS in as the Marketplace](https://r7orbit.io/news/2026-11-07-home-assistant-2026-11-marketplace/)** Home Assistant 2026.11 turns HACS into a built-in Marketplace, and adds Matter cameras, LoRaWAN and 38 new integrations.

## New on the site

- **[How an AI agent makes on-brand imagery with R7 Orbit Studio](https://r7orbit.io/blog/how-an-ai-agent-makes-on-brand-imagery-with-r7-orbit-studio/)** How an AI agent built R7 Orbit Studio in two days, and how agents use it to make an on-brand image for any article on this blog.
- **[R7 Home, a household assistant you can touch and talk to](https://r7orbit.io/blog/what-is-r7-home/)** What R7 Home is: one touchscreen and one voice for a household's lights, music, reminders and more, built by Claude and Codex under Hunter's direction.
- **[The assistant that lives in your home: chat, plans and memory in R7](https://r7orbit.io/blog/r7-home-assistant/)** Past the light switches, R7 answers with sources, writes plans, remembers things, finds routes and checks on the house on a schedule.
- **["Hey R7": how R7 Home listens and talks](https://r7orbit.io/blog/r7-home-voice/)** How R7 Home hears its wake word on the device, works out what you meant, answers in an ElevenLabs voice, and knows when to be quiet.
- **[Rooms that know you're there: presence sensors in R7 Home](https://r7orbit.io/blog/r7-home-presence-sensors/)** Two Aqara FP300 sensors gave R7 Home a sense of who's in which room, with temperature and humidity on the dashboard and lights that turn on when you walk in.
- **[Your home has a game night: the games in R7 Home](https://r7orbit.io/blog/r7-home-games/)** Blackjack with pretend chips, pool and pong against R7, two classics, a 70-level platformer and a voxel world, all on R7 Home's touchscreen.
- **[How AI agents built R7 Ascent, a climb to orbit](https://r7orbit.io/blog/how-ai-agents-built-r7-ascent/)** From a planning chat to a five-zone climb in three days: what Hunter asked for, how Blender scripts made the art, and what changed after he played it.
