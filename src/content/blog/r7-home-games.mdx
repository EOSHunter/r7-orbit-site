## Players and chips

Before playing, you pick a player. Each household member gets a profile with a name, an avatar and their own stats for every game.

The casino games use R7 Chips, which are pretend. Nobody can buy them or cash them out, and no money is involved anywhere. Every new player starts with 10,000 chips and can add more at any time, so a bad night at the blackjack table costs nothing.

## Casino night

- **Blackjack.** A table with up to three seats, so several players can sit down together.
- **R7 Slots.** A 3D slot machine with five paylines.
- **R7 Crash.** A rocket climbs and your winnings climb with it. Cash out before it crashes.

A music button in all three plays a casino playlist through R7's Spotify controls.

## Pool, pong and the classics

These games skip the chips.

- **R7 Pool.** 8-Ball and 9-Ball, against R7 on Easy, Normal or Hard, or against another player.
- **R7 Pong.** A 3D arena where you play R7 or a friend. First to seven wins, but you have to win by two.
- **Minesweeper.** Three difficulties, and the first tap is always safe.
- **Memory.** Six, eight or twelve pairs of cards that flip over in 3D.

Everything works by touch, with no keyboard needed. Two more cards, Snake and a Daily R7 Challenge, are on the page and still say "Coming soon."

## R7: The Last Iteration

The Last Iteration is a platformer made in Godot, a free game engine. A man named Ret wakes up inside a testing facility. R7, the facility's dryly funny AI, guides him through test chambers, because it wants to understand how people solve problems they've never seen before.

There are 70 chambers, and you can play as Ret, as Cody, as Brack (an armored alligator), or as Loki and Thor, "The Beast", a two-headed cat. R7 appears as a little cream-colored robot face beside the subtitles and narrates the whole way in the same ElevenLabs voice R7 Home uses. The lines are recorded ahead of time, so the game never needs the internet to talk.

It plays on the Pi inside R7 Home, and there's a Windows version too. The game is still a prototype.

## R7 World

R7 World is a voxel game, built from blocks you can dig and place. You and R7 arrive at an abandoned field station and set up a foothold, gathering materials, crafting tools and building. R7 is a robot in the world with you, more than a voice in a chat window. You can ask it to go gather timber or stone, and it brings the haul back to you or drops it in the base crate.

It runs on the Pi itself, in Godot, and only there. Its card on the Games page has a Play button that starts it on the Pi, and the robot and the station were built in Blender, a free 3D modeling program. R7 World started on Sep 11, 2026, and is also still a prototype.

## The screen stays awake

While a game is open, R7 Home stops itself from dropping into standby and keeps the screen on, so a long pool game doesn't go dark between shots. The nightly screen-off schedule still wins, though. At bedtime the screen turns off even mid-game, and a tap brings it back for two minutes.

## What's next

Snake and the Daily R7 Challenge are the next cards waiting to be built, and both bigger games are still being worked on.
