This is sample content. It exists to show the optional featured image on a blog post, and to test it.

## How it works

Add `featuredImage` (a file under `src/assets/`) and `featuredImageAlt` to a post's front matter. Leave them out and the post keeps its plain header.

## Why a gradient

The text is always ivory, so the image gets a dark gradient that keeps it readable.
