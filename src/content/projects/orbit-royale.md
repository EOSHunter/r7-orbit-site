Orbit Royale is a first-person sci-fi shooter that you play in the browser. It’s set on Ringfall, an abandoned frontier colony on the planet Veyra, under huge planetary rings. The island has colony towns, a crater lake, jungle, ruins and basalt mountains. You get around by sprinting, sliding, mantling, gliding, driving, and swinging on the Tethercaster grapple.

**Battle Royale:** You and 59 bots jump from a dropship onto Ringfall. You loot guns, grenades and heals from common up to legendary, fight, and outlast an ion storm barrier that keeps closing in. Every player gets three lives while respawns are open, and the last one standing wins.

**Dead Orbit:** A night-time survival mode on the same island, in the style of Call of Duty Zombies. You start at Glass Basin with a pistol and hold off rounds of zombies that keep getting bigger and tougher. Kills earn points, which you spend on opening sealed locations, wall-buy guns, perks, the mystery box, traps and the Rarity Forge. You can survive for as long as you can, or chase the main quest: find the three Ion Core parts, build the Ion Bow and escape.
