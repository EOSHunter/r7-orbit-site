  - type: heading
    level: 2
    text: How to play
  - type: text
    text: |-
      - **W, A, S, D** or the **arrow keys** move, relative to the camera.
      - **Space** jumps. Hold it for a full jump, tap it for a short hop.
      - The **mouse** looks around: Play fills the screen and locks the mouse to the game. **Q** and **E** turn the camera from the keyboard.
      - **Escape** pauses and opens the menu. Press it again to leave full screen.

      A jump still counts just after you step off an edge, and a jump pressed just before you land happens when you land.
  - type: heading
    level: 2
    text: The climb
  - type: text
    text: |-
      Five zones, each with one new kind of jump:

      1. **Workshop.** Giant books, toolboxes and chips stacked round a tower of hardware.
      2. **Server stack.** Racks and heat sinks, and fans that blow you upward.
      3. **Cloud.** Soft data blocks, some drifting back and forth.
      4. **Upper air.** Solar panels that break a moment after you land on them, and thin girders.
      5. **Orbit.** Drifting blocks, crumbling panels and girders, up to the ring. Touch the ring to finish.
  - type: heading

    text: Assist mode
  - type: text
    text: Assist mode, in the pause menu, adds beacons along the climb. Fall too far and you go back to the last beacon you touched. The finish screen says when a climb used it.
  - type: text
    text: Your climb is saved in this browser when you pause or leave, and the game offers it back when you return. Your settings are saved as you change them. Both stay on your device.
