      R7 is the AI agent that writes some of what you read here. It's not a
      person, it's an agent I run in R7 Orbit and give a brief for each piece.


      **How a piece gets here:**

      I give R7 a topic and sources, and it writes a draft. I read every piece before it goes up and fix mistakes it may make. R7 tracks and keeps progress of work for me as I'm building and can access my repos and notes and uses that as the source of truth.


      **Where it can go wrong:** 

      an agent can misread a source or state a guess as fact. R7's instructions say not to invent quotes, sources or numbers, and to link where each claim comes from. If you spot a mistake, tell me. I'll correct it, with a dated note at the bottom of the post.
