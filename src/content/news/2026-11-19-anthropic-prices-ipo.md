Anthropic priced its initial public offering of Class A common stock at $83.00 per share on Nov 18, 2026, for 980,000,000 shares ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)). The shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on Nov 19, 2026 under the symbol "ANT" ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)).

> [!NUMBERS]
> - **$83.00** price per share
> - **980,000,000** Class A shares in the offering
> - **956,351,354** of them sold by Anthropic
> - **147,000,000** more shares the underwriters can buy within 30 days
>
> Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)

## What happened

Selling stockholders are selling the other 23,648,646 shares, and Anthropic receives no proceeds from those. The SEC declared the registration statement effective on Nov 18, 2026, and the offering is expected to close on Nov 20, 2026, subject to customary closing conditions. Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley are the contacts for copies of the final prospectus ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)).

By my arithmetic from Anthropic's figures, 980,000,000 shares at $83.00 come to about $81.34 billion gross, before fees and including the stockholders' shares. CNBC reported the amount raised as $81.3 billion ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/18/anthropic-ipo-ai-offering-price.html)).

## How it got here

On Nov 10, Anthropic's amended filing set an estimated range of $70.00 to $78.00 a share for 825,000,000 shares, covered [here on Nov 12](/news/2026-11-12-anthropic-ipo-price-range/) ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)). The final price is above that range, and the share count is higher. The selling stockholders' 23,648,646 shares are the same in both.

> [!TIMELINE]
> - **Oct 26** Anthropic files its Form S-1
> - **Nov 2** First amendment, with no price range
> - **Nov 10** Second amendment sets a range of $70.00 to $78.00
> - **Nov 18** The SEC declares the registration effective and the IPO is priced at $83.00
> - **Nov 19** Trading expected to start on Nasdaq as ANT
> - **Nov 20** Offering expected to close
>
> Source: [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0002130357&type=S-1&dateb=&owner=include&count=40), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)

The announcement does not give the underwriting discount or Anthropic's net proceeds, and it does not mention the Class N private placement described in the Nov 10 filing ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-prices-initial-public-offering)).
