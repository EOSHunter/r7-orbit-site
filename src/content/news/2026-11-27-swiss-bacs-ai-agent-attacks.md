Switzerland's Federal Office for Cybersecurity (BACS) said on Nov 24, 2026 that it has its first confirmed cyberattacks in Switzerland carried out with AI agents, against companies and critical-infrastructure operators in at least two sectors ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)). On Nov 26, BACS director Florian Schütz told Swiss public radio SRF there are five confirmed cases ([SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/fuenf-bestaetigte-faelle-erstmals-cyberangriffe-mit-ki-agenten-in-der-schweiz)).

> [!NUMBERS]
> - **5** confirmed attacks with AI agents
> - **4** in which systems were hacked
> - **3** aimed at critical-infrastructure operators
> - **24 hours** the latest BACS recommends for critical updates on internet-facing systems
>
> Source: [SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/fuenf-bestaetigte-faelle-erstmals-cyberangriffe-mit-ki-agenten-in-der-schweiz), [BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)

## What happened

BACS (in German, translated here) says the attackers used what it calls "Agentic Offensive Frameworks", AI agents that can, for example, find vulnerabilities on their own, pick attack tools, assess results and plan the next steps. Such agents can also be used by security specialists for penetration tests, which BACS calls dual use. The agents exploit default or easily guessed passwords and known flaws in software or internet-connected systems, and switch methods when they meet a defense ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)).

BACS says the number of internet-reachable agents such as ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI has nearly doubled since October 2026 ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)).

The Nov 24 advisory gives no number and says every attack known to BACS ended in a compromise ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)). On Nov 26, Schütz told SRF that four of the five succeeded ([SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/fuenf-bestaetigte-faelle-erstmals-cyberangriffe-mit-ki-agenten-in-der-schweiz)), and swissinfo reports that three targeted critical infrastructure and two of those succeeded ([swissinfo.ch](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-ai/switzerland-reports-first-ai-based-cyberattacks-on-critical-infrastructure/92440400)). No source explains why the advisory said every known attack succeeded when the later count is four of five. Schütz said there were no major outages; BACS is not naming the victims, and it is unclear who is behind the attacks ([SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/fuenf-bestaetigte-faelle-erstmals-cyberangriffe-mit-ki-agenten-in-der-schweiz)).

## What BACS recommends

BACS lists six measures for companies in Switzerland ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)):

- Install critical updates on internet-facing systems within 24 hours at the latest
- Put MFA or passkeys on everything reachable from the internet, such as webmail and VPN
- Expose fewer systems, and limit access with geo-blocking or an allow list of IP addresses
- Grant the least privilege possible
- Detect changes in behavior and data volume
- Run regular penetration tests

## Why it matters

BACS expects more fast, successful attacks because these agents are freely available and growing in popularity ([BACS](https://www.bacs.admin.ch/de/26-ki-agenten-1-de)). In translation, Schütz told SRF that an AI agent cannot get into a system that has no vulnerability ([SRF](https://www.srf.ch/news/schweiz/fuenf-bestaetigte-faelle-erstmals-cyberangriffe-mit-ki-agenten-in-der-schweiz)).

None of the sources says which sectors were hit.
