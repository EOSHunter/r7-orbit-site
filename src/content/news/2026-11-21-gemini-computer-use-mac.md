Google launched computer use in the Gemini app for Mac on Nov 19, 2026 ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)). You describe a multi-step task, and Gemini does the clicking, typing, menus and forms across your local files, desktop apps and the web.

> [!NOTE]
> Computer use is rolling out in English in the U.S. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS Sequoia 15.0 or later and a personal Google account for ages 18 and up, on Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)).

## What happened

Gemini works in an isolated background window, with a live preview in the chat. Google says your main screen and cursor stay free while it works ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)).

Google lists four controls ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)):

- You choose which apps Gemini can access.
- You can watch every step live.
- You can pause it at any time.
- You approve key actions before they happen.

The post gives four example uses: batch color-grading photos or setting up 3D scenes in design software, copying weights, dimensions and addresses from local PDFs into shipping quote forms, building a Google Slides deck from metrics and screenshots on live dashboards, and staging resale listings from a folder of clothing photos ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)).

## Why it matters

Google says the feature saves time on repetitive work that "normally takes dozens of clicks" ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/computer-use-mac/)). The app is a download at gemini.google/mac.

The post does not say which model runs computer use, what counts as a key action that needs approval, or when it reaches other countries or languages. It does not mention Windows, give prices beyond the plan names, or share any measured results.
