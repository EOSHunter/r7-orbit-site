OpenAI introduced Businesses in ChatGPT in beta on Nov 10, 2026. A company can claim its profile in ChatGPT, keep its details current and, if it is an eligible US ecommerce or online service business, launch a Business Agent that people chat with inside ChatGPT ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

> [!NOTE]
> OpenAI says 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT each week. The post gives no price for Business Hub or for Business Agents ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

## What happened

Business Hub is where a company claims its organization. Once verified, it can update key details and add product feeds with pricing and availability. Any eligible business located where ChatGPT is available can claim a profile. Shopify merchants can connect their store from Business Hub or the Shopify admin ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

Business Hub also shows aggregated insights: how many times a business is mentioned, the website clicks it gets, and discovery topics, the common themes across conversations where its website is cited. OpenAI says it does not share users' conversations or Business Agent chats with businesses, and insights appear only when there are enough conversations to meet certain thresholds ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

A Business Agent is a chat assistant the business builds, tests and launches itself. When a business shows up in an answer, people can open its ChatGPT profile and start a conversation with its agent. OpenAI says that chat is clearly labeled and kept separate from the person's original conversation and from ChatGPT's own answers ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

## Why it matters

OpenAI calls the launch "an early step in bringing businesses more deeply into ChatGPT" and says it plans to "build tools for organizations with more complex needs" ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)). Businesses can also sponsor their agent as the destination for ChatGPT Ads, and OpenAI says sponsored placements are labeled and separate from ChatGPT responses ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).

Business Agents are available only in the United States for now. OpenAI plans to bring them to more markets and business categories over time, and the post gives no dates for that ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/)).
