NVIDIA's board voted unanimously on Nov 23, 2026 to reinstate Jensen Huang as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)). He had resigned on Nov 12 ([covered here on Nov 13](/news/2026-11-13-nvidia-ceo-transition/)).

> [!NOTE]
> The board says it received input from "senior levels of the United States government" and, based on that input, is confident that Huang's reinstatement "poses no national security risk." Harvey C. Jones stays board chair, and Colette Kress, interim CEO since Nov 12, continues as CFO ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)).

> [!TIMELINE]
> - **Nov 8** The board opens an independent investigation
> - **Nov 12** Huang resigns and Kress becomes interim CEO
> - **Nov 23** The board reinstates Huang
>
> Source: [NVIDIA, Nov 8](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors), [NVIDIA, Nov 12](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition), [NVIDIA, Nov 23](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)

## What happened

The board says it voted after a series of discussions and meetings with Huang, after which it "was satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang's candor and ability to lead NVIDIA." Huang said: "I can't wait to get back to work." ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)) TechCrunch reports that board chair Harvey C. Jones said during the company's third-quarter earnings call that the board did not intend to reinstate Huang ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-reverses-course-and-brings-jensen-huang-back-as-ceo/)).

The release does not name President Trump. Both the AP and TechCrunch report that Trump publicly backed Huang's return and said the attorney general confirmed the government has no evidence that Huang engaged in unlawful conduct ([AP, via PBS News](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/nvidia-board-unanimously-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo-and-chairman), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-reverses-course-and-brings-jensen-huang-back-as-ceo/)). The AP reports that Trump told CNBC, "I believe my intervention was very important" ([AP, via PBS News](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/nvidia-board-unanimously-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo-and-chairman)).

The release does not say which officials gave input or what they said.

## What comes next

The board says the independent committee's investigation, opened on Nov 8 ([covered here](/news/2026-11-08-nvidia-board-investigation-jensen-huang/)), "will proceed to conclusion," and that Huang supports it. The board has also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee of independent directors, and NVIDIA is starting a search to fill a new position, Chief Compliance Officer ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director)). The AP reports that Huang is scheduled to sit for an interview next week with three House committees ([AP, via PBS News](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/nvidia-board-unanimously-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-ceo-and-chairman)).
