Adobe and The Walt Disney Company announced a multi-year strategic alliance on Nov 9, 2026, at Adobe MAX, with Adobe named an Official AI Innovation Partner of The Walt Disney Company ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)). Adobe's tools will support concept work in Disney's innovation labs and in its parks and cruise development pipelines ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)).

> [!NOTE]
> Adobe will give Disney early access to its latest AI, creative and media tools, with dedicated engineering and research resources, to support the Disney production pipeline ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)).

## What Disney gets

The release names four products ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)):

- **Adobe Firefly**, generative AI for creatives
- **Adobe Firefly Foundry**, proprietary AI models
- **Adobe Premiere**, video editing
- **Adobe Photoshop**, digital imaging

With Firefly Foundry, Disney will work with Adobe on generative AI models customized on Disney properties and trained on character attributes, environments and visual styles. Adobe says the models will produce "commercially safe and production-ready outputs" ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)). Becca Vodnoy, senior vice president, Corporate Alliances, The Walt Disney Company, said the alliance will "give our creative teams new ways to explore and develop their ideas" ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)).

## What Imagineering already uses

Walt Disney Imagineering Research & Development already uses Firefly Foundry in theme park development. It has a sketch-to-image model that turns hand-drawn concepts into fully rendered 2D concept art, and a custom image model that generates on-brand, franchise-accurate creative assets ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)).

## StudioLAB and ESPN Edge

| Group | What it is | Adobe's part |
| --- | --- | --- |
| StudioLAB | The Walt Disney Studios' innovation hub, where creative and technical staff prototype ideas | Joins as a StudioLAB Innovation Partner and works with Disney creatives and technologists on internal creative workflows |
| ESPN Edge | ESPN's innovation center for testing new technology in sports storytelling | Looks with ESPN for ways AI can engage sports fans, shape how ESPN delivers content and bring ESPN's archive to life |

From [Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance). Projects with both groups are already underway.

## What else is in it

Adobe will promote its work with Walt Disney World Resort, Disneyland Resort, Disney Cruise Line, ESPN Edge and StudioLAB through marketing, industry events and customer stories, and will increase its presence across Disney's media platforms ([Adobe](https://news.adobe.com/news/2026/11/adobe-and-the-walt-disney-company-alliance)). The release gives no dollar value and no length in years, and does not say whether the alliance is exclusive.
