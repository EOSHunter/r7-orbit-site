Anthropic set an estimated price range of $70.00 to $78.00 per share for its initial public offering in an amended Form S-1 filed with the SEC on Nov 10, 2026 ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)). It is offering 825,000,000 shares of Class A common stock and has applied to list them on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as "ANT" ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)).

> [!NUMBERS]
> - **$70.00 to $78.00** estimated price per share
> - **825,000,000** Class A shares offered
> - **$26.0 billion** of Class N stock in a private placement
> - **$84.6 billion** estimated net proceeds at $74.00
>
> Source: [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)

## What the filing sets

This is Anthropic's second amendment, after the S-1 of Oct 26 and a first amendment on Nov 2 ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0002130357&type=S-1&dateb=&owner=include&count=40)). The Nov 2 version, covered [here on Nov 8](/news/2026-11-08-anthropic-ipo-amended-filing/), had no price range or share count.

Anthropic is selling 801,351,354 of the shares, and selling stockholders are selling the other 23,648,646, with no proceeds from those going to Anthropic. The underwriters can buy up to 123,750,000 more shares from Anthropic. At the $74.00 midpoint, Anthropic estimates net proceeds of about $84.6 billion from the offering and the private placement ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)).

## The private placement

Seven companies agreed to buy $26.0 billion of Anthropic's Class N common stock at the IPO price, in a private placement set to close right after the offering ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)).

> [!CHART] Class N purchases at $74.00 a share
> - Google: $10.0 billion
> - NVIDIA: $10.0 billion
> - AMD: $2.5 billion
> - Broadcom: $1.0 billion
> - Micron: $1.0 billion
> - Microsoft: $1.0 billion
> - TSMC: $0.5 billion
>
> Source: [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)

## Control, and what is still blank

The Class F stock, held by the Founder LLC, will carry 50.1% of the total voting power on matters put to stockholders generally, including the election of directors other than the Class T directors, and Anthropic says it is a "controlled company" under Nasdaq's governance rules. The final price, the underwriting discount and the delivery date are still blank, and the filing says the preliminary prospectus "is not complete and may be changed" ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026072484/anthropicpbc-sx1ax2.htm)).

Fortune reported on Nov 10 that Anthropic had launched its roadshow, that trading could start as early as the Wednesday or Thursday of the following week, and that the range would value the company at up to $1.4 trillion on a fully diluted basis ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-ipo-price-range-ai-stock-sell-off/)). The filing itself gives no trading date and no valuation.
