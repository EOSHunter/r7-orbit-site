Anthropic filed to go public on Oct 26, 2026 and plans to list its Class A common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "ANT" ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec)).

> [!CHART] Revenue for the three months to Sep 30
> - 2025: $1.38 billion
> - **2026: $16.96 billion**
>
> Source: the amended filing of Nov 2, as read by [Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/anthropic-ipo-amended-s1-2026-guide).

## What happened

SEC records list Anthropic's Form S-1 on Oct 26 and the amended S-1/A on Nov 2 ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0002130357&type=S-1)). When it filed, Anthropic said the number of shares and the price range "have not yet been determined" ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec)). Stock Titan reports that the amendment still leaves the date, the price range and the number of shares blank ([Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/anthropic-ipo-amended-s1-2026-guide)).

Copies of the preliminary prospectus can be had, when available, from Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec)). Stock Titan reports revenue of $33.2 billion for the first nine months of 2026 ([Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/anthropic-ipo-amended-s1-2026-guide)).

## Why it matters

Control of the board stays with Anthropic's trust and founders. According to Stock Titan, the filing gives the Long-Term Benefit Trust one Class T share that elects most of the directors, and a founders' company one Class F share carrying 50.1% of the vote. Together they "will have the ability to elect all members of our board of directors," the filing says, as quoted by Stock Titan ([Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/anthropic-ipo-amended-s1-2026-guide)).

No listing date, price or valuation has been filed. Stock Titan works out Nov 10 as the earliest date a roadshow could start under SEC staff practice; that is its own calculation, not a date Anthropic has announced ([Stock Titan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/anthropic-ipo-amended-s1-2026-guide)).
