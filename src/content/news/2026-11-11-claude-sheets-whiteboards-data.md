Anthropic launched Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta on Nov 10, 2026, and made Claude Dashboards generally available under a new name, Claude Data ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)). All three are on every paid plan and work on web, desktop and mobile ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

## What happened

**Whiteboards** is a shared board where a team brainstorms with Claude. Anthropic's example is a retro meeting: everyone adds notes, and Claude groups them by theme and drafts action items for each person. A finished board can become a doc or a deck, or move to Lucid, Miro or Vani by Zoho ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

**Sheets** is a spreadsheet Claude builds and edits with you, with tabs, formulas, formatting and drop-downs. Claude outlines each cell it changes so you can track its edits. Anthropic calls this "simple spreadsheet functionality" for now; files export to .xlsx or CSV, or continue in Airtable, Asana, monday.com and Smartsheet ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

**Claude Data** asks questions of a company's data through a data connector, such as a warehouse or CRM. This release adds certified queries that the data team approves, data stories, design systems, caching with a refresh rate per dashboard, and tracked changes with rollback. Certified queries and live sharing are still in beta. Partners include Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog and Sigma ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

The same day, interactive connectors started opening as full apps beside the conversation or in full screen, for tools such as Adobe, Atlassian, Box and Notion ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)).

## For admins

On Team plans all three are on by default. On Enterprise plans Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default. Admins change these in Organization settings > Artifacts ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/sheets-whiteboards-data)). The article gives no price beyond "all paid plans" and no date for Whiteboards or Sheets to leave beta.
