Google made Gemini 4 Carbon, which it calls its strongest model, available to developers, enterprises, scientists and consumers on Nov 10, 2026, at the same introductory API price as Gemini 4 Argon. That price runs until Nov 30, 2026, and then rises to $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)). Cyber defenders in Google's Fairwind Program got it two weeks earlier.

> [!NUMBERS]
> - **$2** per million input tokens, introductory
> - **$10** per million output tokens, introductory
> - **$4** per million input tokens after the introductory period
> - **$20** per million output tokens after the introductory period
>
> Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)

## What happened

Paying developers and enterprises can call Carbon through the API in Google AI Studio and Gemini for business. It is rolling out to Google AI Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity, the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)).

Google says Argon remains the right choice for most software engineering and knowledge work. It positions Carbon for long, multi-step coding, debugging and open-ended research, and says Carbon uses more tokens than Argon to explore and run longer checks on its own work ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)).

Google is also previewing /swarm in Google Antigravity. One prompt becomes a coordinated team of agents you can follow in real time. Developers can give Carbon the roles that need the most reasoning, such as architecture, coordination and auditing, and pair it with faster, lighter models working in parallel isolated workspaces, with adversarial verifiers testing every change ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)).

> [!CHART] Agentic honesty benchmark, tasks passed
> - Gemini 4 Argon: 70%
> - **Gemini 4 Carbon: 92%**
>
> Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/), Google's own 375-task benchmark.

## Why it matters

For people building with agents, Carbon costs $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens on the API until Nov 30, though Google says it spends more tokens per task. In Google's own safety testing, Carbon's safeguards lowered the per-attempt breach rate in simulated cyberattacks to 0.8%, from 3.1% on Argon. Google also says prompt injection robustness "remains an area for continued improvement" ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)).

Google says the introductory period ends Nov 30, 2026, and that the $4 and $20 rates that follow apply to the Carbon model intended for public access ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/)). Google gives no date for /swarm leaving preview.
