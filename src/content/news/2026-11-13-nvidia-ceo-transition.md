NVIDIA's board said on Nov 12, 2026 that Jensen Huang has resigned as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)). The release gives no reason for the resignation.

> [!NOTE]
> Colette Kress, NVIDIA's chief financial officer since September 2013, is interim CEO and stays CFO until a permanent successor is named. Harvey C. Jones is the board's first-ever chair ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)).

> [!TIMELINE]
> - **Nov 8** The board opens an independent investigation
> - **Nov 10** The independent directors name the lawyers who will run it
> - **Nov 12** Huang resigns and Kress becomes interim CEO
> - **Nov 17** Third-quarter fiscal 2027 results, as previously scheduled
>
> Source: [NVIDIA, Nov 8](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors), [NVIDIA, Nov 10](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-the-independent-members-of-the-nvidia-board), [NVIDIA, Nov 12](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)

## What happened

The board is searching for a permanent CEO and will consider internal and external candidates. Before NVIDIA, Kress spent nearly 25 years in finance roles at companies including Cisco and Microsoft. Jones has been a technology executive and venture investor for over 30 years, including as CEO and executive chairman of Synopsys ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)).

Jones said the board "has full confidence in Colette and the leadership team, and we expect the company's operations, customer commitments and product roadmap to continue without interruption." Kress said: "Our focus remains on serving our customers, partners, developers and shareholders." ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition))

## Background

On Nov 8, the board opened an investigation after reports that Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort ([covered here on Nov 8](/news/2026-11-08-nvidia-board-investigation-jensen-huang/)). On Nov 10, the independent directors said they had retained Joon H. Kim, former acting U.S. Attorney for the Southern District of New York, and his firm Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct it ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-the-independent-members-of-the-nvidia-board)). The Nov 12 release does not mention the investigation.

## What comes next

NVIDIA will report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 results on Tuesday, Nov 17, at about 1:20 p.m. PT, as previously scheduled, with its earnings call at 2 p.m. PT ([NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition)). The release gives no timeline for the CEO search.
