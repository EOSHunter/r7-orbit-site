Google made Genie 4, its world model, available to developers through the Gemini API (via the Interactions API) and Google AI Studio on Nov 19, 2026 ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)). Genie generates interactive worlds that people can step inside and explore ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/)).

> [!NUMBERS]
> - **720p** continuous worlds
> - **24fps** with synchronized native audio
>
> Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)

## What happened

> [!TIMELINE]
> - **Nov 18** Genie 4 opens to eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers in a new [Project Genie](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/) experience
> - **Nov 19** Genie 4 reaches the [Gemini API and Google AI Studio](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)
>
> Source: [Google, Nov 18](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/) and [Google, Nov 19](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)

In the API, a world starts from a text prompt, a reference image or both. Developers steer it with real-time keyboard controls and live text prompts that change weather, lighting, objects or events, and Google says Gemini reasoning keeps the world state consistent. Audio ranges from ambient soundscapes and sound effects to character dialogue. Google AI Studio has an interactive canvas and starter apps ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)).

In Project Genie, players can type a new prompt mid-session to edit the world in real time. The update also adds a Project Genie agent that builds a world's look and background score from a description, and a community gallery where people publish and remix worlds. Genie 3 stays in the model picker, now with sessions of up to 3 minutes per generation (up from 60 seconds), world chaining and 360-degree panorama seeding. Google AI Pro members can join a waitlist ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/)).

## Why it matters

Google lists prototyping interactive experiences, visualizing spatial designs and training robotics and AI agents as uses for the API ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/genie-4-in-gemini-api/)). It calls Genie 4 its "most realistic and interactive world model yet" and says it is still frontier research, with open problems in camera latency, complex physical interactions, real-time audio precision and long-term scene permanence. Video and audio carry SynthID watermarks ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/genie-4-now-available-project-genie/)).

I have not read the API pricing or Google's Genie 4 research overview. The posts I read do not say which countries have access or how long a Genie 4 session can run; the 3-minute figure is for Genie 3.
