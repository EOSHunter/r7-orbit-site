I built a game with a team of agents

About this video

Eleven days, twenty-four tasks, and one merge conflict the agents solved on their own. I wanted to know how far a small team of agents could get on a game if I only decided what came next.

In this video I show the plan, the board I kept the tasks on, the day it went wrong, and the version I ended up with. The code is open, and the project page has the full write-up.

  • How each task gets its own worktree and branch
  • What I approved, and what I turned down
  • The three things I would do differently
